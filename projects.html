NSF RAPID: Hurricane Harvey Zooplankton Sampling

PI: Hui Liu | NSF Award 1760463 | 2017-2019

This project supported urgent monthly sampling of zooplankton and water properties along the Galveston Bay salinity gradient after Hurricane Harvey. The work documented post-storm pelagic community dynamics, hydrographic conditions, and ecosystem recovery signals relevant to larval fish habitat and estuarine restoration.
